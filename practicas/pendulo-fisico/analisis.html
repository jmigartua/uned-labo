Mechanics Practice 1: Physical Pendulum

Physics Lab (1st Course)

2026-10-29

Objective

Determine the radius of gyration k and the local gravitational acceleration g using a physical pendulum (uniform rod), with:

  • Rod length: L = 0.66\ \mathrm{m}
  • Length measurement precision: 1\ \mathrm{mm}
  • Chronometer precision for 20T: 0.01\ \mathrm{s}

Plot 1: T vs b

Linearized Model

For a physical pendulum:

T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{k^2 + b^2}{g\,b}}

Equivalent linear form:

bT^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{g}b^2 + \frac{4\pi^2}{g}k^2

Define:

X = b^2, \qquad Y = bT^2

Then:

Y = mX + c, \quad m = \frac{4\pi^2}{g}, \quad c = \frac{4\pi^2}{g}k^2

Regression (Fit-Only Uncertainties)

Fit-only results (1\sigma):

  • m = (4.042341 \pm 0.022915)\ \mathrm{s^2\,m^{-1}}
  • c = (0.138329 \pm 0.001154)\ \mathrm{m\,s^2}
  • k = (0.184987 \pm 0.001232)\ \mathrm{m}
  • g = (9.7662 \pm 0.0554)\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}
  • R^2 = 0.999615
